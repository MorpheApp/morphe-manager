# Morphe guides

Step by step guides for using Morphe Manager. If you are new here, start with the first one.

## 🩹 Patching

- **[Patching an app in Simple mode](patching-simple-mode.md)** - your first patch, from
  picking an app to installing the result. No configuration needed.
- **[The home screen and managing apps](home-screen.md)** - sorting, grouping, hiding, and
  bulk actions on your app list.
- **[Patching an app in Expert mode](patching-expert-mode.md)** - choosing individual
  patches, configuring their options, and reading the patcher log.
- **[Choosing how patched apps are installed](installers.md)** - the system installer,
  Shizuku, root mount, and the Play Store install source.
- **[Updating a patched app](updating-patched-apps.md)** - what the Update badge means, and
  how to repatch without losing app data.
- **[Managing patch sources](patch-sources.md)** - adding remote and local sources, and what
  the toggles on a source card do.
- **[How patching works and how to tune it](patching-process.md)** - the steps of a run, the
  process runtime and memory limit, and what to do when patching fails.

## 🎨 Customization

- **[Creating a custom app icon](custom-app-icon.md)** - a custom launcher icon and app
  name for the patched app, in both Simple and Expert mode.
- **[Creating a custom header logo](custom-header-logo.md)** - replacing the logo at the top
  of the patched app, for the light and dark theme.

## 🧰 Maintenance

- **[Backing up Morphe and your keystore](backup-and-keystore.md)** - why the keystore
  matters, and how to move everything to another device.
- **[Storage and saved data](storage-and-saved-data.md)** - what Morphe keeps on your device,
  what is safe to clear, and how saved patch selections work.
- **[Using the built-in file picker](file-picker.md)** - Morphe's own file browser, for when
  the system picker gets in the way.
